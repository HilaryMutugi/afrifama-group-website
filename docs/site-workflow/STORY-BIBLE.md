## 8. Voice and tone rules

- Warm, confident and plain. Short sentences.
- Specific over generic. Name the place, the stage, the number.
- No hype words: leading, innovative, world-class, cutting-edge, revolutionary.
- Honest about what is still being built.
- No em dashes.
- Swahili: OPEN, do I want a few Swahili words used, and where?

## 9. Words we use, words we avoid

Use: farmer, flock, feed, system, partnership, layer cycle, readiness.
Avoid: beneficiaries (unless a funder requires it), guarantee, donation, loan (the site already states support is recoverable, not a grant and not a conventional loan).

## 10. Things we must never claim

- Any guarantee of profit or income for farmers.
- Any partnership, certification or licence we do not hold.
- Any capacity, volume or date we cannot deliver.
- Anything about the parent stock supply chain that is not signed and confirmed.

OPEN: Add anything else.

## 11. Photography and image direction

- Keep the recurring farmer as an adult smallholder farmer in his early-to-mid thirties. Afrifama serves farmers across age groups and must not appear youth-exclusive.
- Dress the farmer in practical, unbranded farm clothing and boots. Reserve Afrifama-branded clothing for staff, such as a technical-support officer.
- Keep farms recognisably coastal Kenyan, with practical poultry housing, believable flock sizes and natural vegetation.
- Prefer neutral daylight or restrained warm light. Avoid heavily saturated sunsets, theatrical skies and overly polished farm environments.
