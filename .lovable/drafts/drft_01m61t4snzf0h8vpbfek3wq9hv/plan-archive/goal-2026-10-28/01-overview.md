## Goal
Rework only the About Afrifama page into a warmer, people-first story that farmers, partners and investors can trust. It should be rooted in Kilifi County and open about Afrifama being an early-growth startup. No new figures, testimonials, partnerships or milestones.

## The story, in order
1. **Hero:** a short, human headline about farming on Kenya's coast. One sentence says who Afrifama is (an early-growth agribusiness in Kilifi County) and one button scrolls down to the story. The photo space stays a placeholder.
2. **The challenge farmers face:** the existing sunrise "farmer's morning" scene, tightened. Then three short, concrete pressures, written as plain lines rather than cards: inputs that are hard to afford, little support when birds fall sick, and no dependable buyer.
3. **Why Afrifama exists:** a large pull-quote ("Farming should not feel like a gamble"), followed by one short paragraph.
4. **What we are building:** feeds, farmer partnerships, poultry production support and market coordination, shown as one connected line. Genetics & Hatchery is clearly labelled "In development".
5. **How we work with people:** four short commitments. We explain clearly, agree support in writing, keep farm records private and respect farmers as business owners. The current "Input financing" wording becomes "recoverable production support, agreed in writing" so it matches the farmer partnership rules (it is not a loan or a donation).
6. **Honest about where we are:** a calm "Early-growth startup" panel. It covers what is underway (the pilot) and what is still being built, using only the figures the site already shows with their "as of" date. If those figures aren't verified for this page, the panel stays wording-only.
7. **Closing:** one invitation with three clear paths: farmers go to Farmer Partnership, partners and investors go to Contact, and a quieter text link goes to Our Businesses.

## Reading, type and mobile
- Text lines stay at about 60 characters, with short paragraphs and a single heading size per level.
- Section backgrounds alternate cream, white and deep green, and the numbered labels are removed.
- The four repeated icon cards and the stray "Generated illustration" caption are removed.
- On phones, text comes before each photo space, headings are no more than about 3 lines, buttons are full-width and stacked, and there is no sideways scrolling.
- Existing spacing and reduced-motion rules are kept, with gentle fade-ins only.
