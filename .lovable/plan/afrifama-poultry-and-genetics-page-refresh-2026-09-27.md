# Afrifama Poultry and Genetics Page Refresh

## Scope
Redesign only the **Poultry** and **Genetics & Hatchery** pages. Preserve the existing Afrifama header, footer, colour system, typography, navigation, and overall brand character.

## Poultry page
- Introduce a stronger image-led opening using the existing poultry photography.
- Present the operating model as a clear connected system: flock production, Afrifama feeds, facilities and biosecurity, records, and partner-farmer support.
- Use full-width editorial sections and a compact process diagram rather than a grid of oversized cards.
- Add simple pathways for farmers, customers, and partners using existing public pages and honest current capabilities.
- Keep all current claims conservative and remove no existing disclosure about unverified figures.

## Genetics & Hatchery page
- Build the requested story sequence:
  1. The genetics behind a productive flock
  2. Our parent-stock direction
  3. What we are building
  4. Genetics, nutrition and management must work together
  5. Technical partnerships
  6. Future breed profiles and management resources
  7. Partner with Afrifama
- Keep “In Development” prominent throughout and state clearly that Afrifama does not yet operate a completed hatchery or offer commercial chicks.
- Replace the current implied egg-to-chick production sequence with a capability-development diagram.
- Show breed profiles and technical resources as clearly future, unavailable modules—without breed names or performance figures.

## Shared presentation
- Contemporary East African agribusiness tone with warm cream, deep forest, muted gold, and restrained terracotta.
- Use existing images only; no copied competitor media and no new invented photography.
- Use concise editorial layouts, fine rules, compact technical labels, and limited card styling.
- Ensure both pages work cleanly on mobile and desktop with reduced-motion support.

## Content and verification

- Verify both pages visually at desktop and mobile sizes, check links, and confirm the preview builds without errors.
