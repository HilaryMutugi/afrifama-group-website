# Afrifama About page story redesign

## Outcome
Rebuild only `/about` as a concise, image-led company story rooted in Mariakani and coastal Kenya, while preserving the site navigation, footer, palette and factual safeguards.

## Build
- Replace the current generic hero with the supplied headline, supporting statement and a credited documentary photograph.
- Centralise the supplied origin story, 2024–NEXT timeline, approved proof figures, mission, direction, principles and closing statement in the shared content source.
- Compose the page as alternating cream, white and deep-green editorial sections with three to four varied documentary images and clear captions.
- Add a responsive visual timeline with restrained motion and a custom Afrifama system mural showing the operating chain and the future genetics foundation.
- Remove leadership placeholders, business-unit cards and other unfinished About-page content.
- Finish with one “Explore Our Businesses” action.

## Technical details
- Keep claims and captions in `src/content/site.ts`.
- Build the mural as a reusable semantic-token illustration in `src/components/site/`.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve keyboard access, heading order and meaningful alt text.
- Verify the complete page at desktop and mobile sizes, check the preview build, and do not publish.
